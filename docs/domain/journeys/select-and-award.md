# Select recipients and establish awards

The funder chooses what to support, on what terms, within available resources.
Program staff, reviewers, decision-makers, finance colleagues, and authorized
signatories contribute different [responsibilities](../people.md).

## Assess and decide

Eligibility and completeness differ from the quality of the proposed work.
Reviewers assess substance and evidence; financial review examines the budget.
[US federal pre-award guidance](https://grants.gov/learn-grants/grants-101/pre-award-phase)
describes these activities separately and addresses reviewer conflicts of interest.
Their depth and sequence vary by program.

People seek clarification, discuss judgments, and recommend amounts or conditions.
At the [John Templeton Foundation](https://www.templeton.org/grants/apply-for-grant),
staff evaluate proposals while the president or trustees authorize funding.
Conditional approval can require revisions. Assessment, recommendation, decision,
and outstanding conditions therefore carry different meanings.

A strong proposal can lose out when demand exceeds available funding. Communicating
the outcome lets unsuccessful applicants reconsider their plans; see the
[applicant journey](seek-funding.md) for the consequences of rejection and limited
feedback.

## Establish the commitment

The parties agree the supported work, amount, responsibilities, and payment and
reporting terms. The [Halle Foundation](https://thehallefoundation.org/application-process-and-guidelines/)
separately notifies applicants of trustee action and sends successful applicants
an agreement to sign. In [US federal grants](https://grants.gov/learn-grants/grants-101/award-phase),
the Notice of Award is the official, legally binding award; acceptance can occur through signing
or drawing funds. A favorable decision, agreement, acceptance, and payment are
distinct events, even when an institution combines some steps.

The award passes to the people who will [manage delivery and funding](deliver-and-manage.md),
with its obligations and remaining conditions understood.

Questions to investigate: Who can commit each party? What happens if the applicant
cannot accept the amount or conditions? What must be resolved before work or
payment starts?
