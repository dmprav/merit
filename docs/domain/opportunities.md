# Opportunities to investigate

These questions identify problems to investigate across the grant lifecycle.

| Area                                                       | Questions to investigate                                                                                             |
| ---------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| [Finding suitable support](journeys/seek-funding.md)       | How do applicants judge fit before preparing a proposal? Where do eligible organizations leave the process, and why? |
| Application and reporting effort                           | Which information is repeatedly recreated? Which requests help someone make a decision?                              |
| [Decision handoffs](journeys/select-and-award.md)          | Where do staff or applicants lose the reasoning behind a decision? Who needs to understand it next?                  |
| [Changing commitments](journeys/deliver-and-manage.md)     | How do delivery and finance teams learn that plans, payments, or obligations have changed?                           |
| [Learning from funded work](journeys/learn-and-improve.md) | What changes after a report or evaluation? Whose experience is missing?                                              |

CEP's [2023 grantee survey comparison](https://cep.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/07/Before_and_After_2020.pdf)
covered 61 US funders surveyed before 2020 and again in 2021 or 2022. Median
proposal time fell from 20 to 15 hours; annual monitoring, reporting, and evaluation
time fell from eight to six hours. The findings show reduced effort among grantees
of funders that repeatedly commissioned feedback. Unsuccessful applicants were
outside the sample.
