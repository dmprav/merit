# People and responsibilities

These profiles describe work and information needs. One person may hold several
responsibilities, or a team may share them; job titles and software permissions
vary.

| Responsibility                           | Goal                                                               | Needs                                                                                   |
| ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Funder program lead                      | Turn a funding purpose into a workable program and portfolio.      | Priorities, resources, assessment advice, and lessons from funded work.                 |
| Grants administrator                     | Keep applications and awards moving.                               | Clear records, missing information, approvals, agreements, contacts, and deadlines.     |
| Applicant or proposal lead               | Obtain support for work the organization can deliver.              | Funding fit, eligibility, costs, partner commitments, and decision timing.              |
| Reviewer or adviser                      | Give a fair assessment within their expertise.                     | Criteria, evidence, declared conflicts, and clarity about their authority.              |
| Decision-maker                           | Choose what to support and explain trade-offs.                     | Assessments, resources, strategic fit, risks, and authority to commit funding.          |
| Recipient delivery lead                  | Deliver the funded work and adapt to changes.                      | Agreed expectations, resources, team responsibilities, and routes for raising problems. |
| Finance staff                            | Plan cash and expenditure, and account for funding.                | Budgets, payment terms, expenditure records, financial reports, and approved changes.   |
| Authorized organizational representative | Commit the organization within their authority.                    | Submission approvals, obligations, delegated authority, and agreement terms.            |
| Learning or evaluation lead              | Understand results and help people act on findings.                | Questions, reliable evidence, context, recipient experience, and decisions to inform.   |
| Community members and people affected    | Influence priorities and judge whether work addresses their needs. | Accessible participation, clarity about their influence, and feedback on what changed.  |

The profiles draw on [UKRI's separation of project leadership, organizational
approval, and support](https://www.ukri.org/publications/roles-in-funding-applications/roles-in-funding-applications-eligibility-responsibilities-and-costings-guidance/),
[Wellcome's distinction between advice and funding authority](https://wellcome.org/research-funding/guidance/how-wellcome-makes-funding-decisions/how-wellcome-assesses-funding-applications),
and the [Trust-Based Philanthropy Project's call for recipient and community
influence](https://www.trustbasedphilanthropy.org/practices).

## Relationships to understand

Gathering information, approving a change, and answering for the result may fall
to different people. Partnerships add coordination between organizations as well
as with the funder.
