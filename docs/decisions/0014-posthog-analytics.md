# Collect analytics for a defined purpose

Accepted 14 November 2026. Revised 21 November 2026.

Use [PostHog](https://posthog.com/) to understand how people carry out grant work
and where the product falls short. Let the question determine what to collect.
Record actions and outcomes by default. Keep vendor-specific collection behind
the [analytics foundation](../../foundation/analytics/README.md).

Link a person's activity across visits only when the question depends on knowing
they returned. Sample workspace content only when answering the question requires
reading the work itself. Grant material and judgments can remain confidential
even after names are removed. Before collecting a sample, establish what the
organization permits, who needs to read it, and when to delete it. Revisit those
limits before using it for another purpose.
