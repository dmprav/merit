# Verification record

## Behavioral decisions

- Separators have two strengths. `--border-subtle` (a 55% mix of `--border` into `--surface`) is for separation inside a surface: `Divider` by default, Stack `divided="between"`, DescriptionList `divided`, and the tab rail. `--border` stays for surface edges (shell, frame and pane rules, popovers, fields, tables, page-header hairline). `Divider strength="edge"` exists for those.
- `Divider spacing` (default 6) sets equal visible space above and below the rule, whatever the parent Stack's gap. `--parent-stack-gap` is registered with `inherits: false`, so a Divider inside a wrapper keeps its spacing.
- The action-coloured `emphasis` tone is removed. In the compare view both captions share the muted caption style, and their text ("Recorded material (Version N)") identifies the recorded side. No new `Text` variant was added.
- Stack `divided="after"` and `"around"` are removed; they had no product callers.
- Lists separate entries with between-rules and never end with a trailing rule. Assessment sections (latest, awaiting, request history) are separated by headings and spacing, not rules. The history view keeps `--space-4` under the reviewer row.
- Callers whose Divider sits in a Stack pass `spacing` equal to the stack gap, so their rhythm is unchanged. The compare view uses the default 6.
- There is no labelled divider variant, since no caller needs one.
