# Verification record

## Behavioral decisions

- A new `Toggletip`:
  - previews its text on hover or keyboard focus after the shared 350ms delay, and closes after 150ms;
  - pins open on press or tap, and a second press unpins it;
  - stays open while the pointer is over the text, including the move back to the trigger (WCAG 1.4.13);
  - closes on Escape (caught in the capture phase, so a surrounding dialog stays open) or a press elsewhere;
  - is a labelled non-modal `role="group"` on the shared popover surface, with the trigger referencing it through `aria-controls` and `aria-describedby` while open;
  - its tone picks the icon from the shared map.
- `StatusIndicator` keeps its API but renders a Toggletip, so its explanation shows once instead of in a tooltip and a popover.
- Reviewer pane header:
  - the due date is a `MetaItem`: calendar icon, visually hidden "Response requested by" prefix, muted `CalendarDate`, no tone change (owner's choice);
  - with no due date it's omitted;
  - one Toggletip shows who requested the review and the privacy or amendment note on separate lines, without repeating the date.
- `CalendarDate` (`<time dateTime>`, exported from `@merit/ui/timestamp`) replaces bare dates in metadata positions: due, submitted on, committee date, assessment date, recommendation meta. `MetaItem` (16px icon, muted `--text-detail`, `--meta-gap`) and `Attribution` share one style.
- `Badge` is status-only:
  - `children: string`;
  - plain badges draw their tone icon for success, warning and danger;
  - a neutral plain badge may pass an `icon`, which other tones ignore.
  - "Read only" in the funding round view became muted supporting text.
- One `toneIcons` map (`@merit/ui/icon`) feeds Notice, Toast, Badge and Toggletip. The `warning` icon name is replaced by `attention`/`failure`. Domain icon names are renamed: `round` to `calendar`, `application` to `document`, `team` to `people`, `contacts` to `addressBook`.
