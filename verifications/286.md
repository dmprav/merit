# Verification record

## Behavioral decisions

- The reviewer assignment read returns `work`. It is available, or blocked with `action_unavailable` (not accepted, or access paused or ended) or `application_withdrawn`. Saves and submissions use the same decision when they commit. A replayed submission receipt still succeeds after withdrawal.
- On a withdrawn application, a submitted reviewer keeps the material and every submitted version. The screen offers no amendment, editor, conflict report or withdraw action. It shows a notice with a link to contact the manager, and unsaved writing stays available to copy.
- When access lapses for membership, role or organization reasons while the status is still accepted or submitted, a refresh now shows unsaved writing to copy instead of retrying autosave.
