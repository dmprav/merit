# Verification record

## Behavioral decisions

- A navigation at any point in the focus check reports `trackerLost` and keeps the stops reached before it. The requester asked for every step to be covered, not only tracker installation.
- A tracker lost while capturing the final or nothing-focused baselines reports `trackerLost` rather than a complete traversal.
- The tracker is not reinstalled on the new document, so the focus results keep describing the document the audit and screenshot describe.
