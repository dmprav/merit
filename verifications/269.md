# Verification record

## Behavioral decisions

- Overlays (centered dialog, side panel, reference popover, companion sheet) show header and footer edges only while content scrolls under them. The docked companion panel and workspace pane headers keep a rule at rest because they continue the shell's top-bar line.
- Below 30rem viewport height, a centered dialog or side panel scrolls as a whole; otherwise only the body scrolls. The companion sheet keeps its header and ask box fixed at every height.
- At any height, a centered dialog or side panel keeps at least 4rem for its body; when enlarged text, a long title, or long feedback leave less, the whole frame scrolls instead of clipping fields and actions (CSS only, replacing the removed content measurement).
- Workspace panes always render a header bar, falling back to the pane `label`. Pane `titleActions` appear only with a `title`.
- Every frame uses one title style (`--text-body`, `--weight-heading`). This shrinks the centered dialog title from `--text-reading`.
- The companion panel defaults to 28rem, matching the side panel.
- In stacked pane layout, pane and sticky form footers keep a plain top rule, because the page scrolls rather than the pane.
- `FrameFooter` is exported from `@merit/ui/dialog` for `@merit/forms`.
