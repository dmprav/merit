# Verification record

## Behavioral decisions

- `InlineName`:
  - edits in place in exactly the name's box, using an auto-growing `<textarea rows=1>` that shares an `inline-grid` cell with a `white-space: pre-wrap` mirror, so long names wrap identically in view and edit;
  - Enter saves without inserting a newline, and pasted newlines become spaces;
  - blur saves; an unchanged or empty draft reverts without saving; Escape reverts and refocuses the trigger;
  - pending makes the field read-only with `aria-busy` and shows a spinner in the trailing slot;
  - failure keeps the draft and shows a `role="alert"` caption referenced by `aria-describedby` with `aria-invalid`, overlaid within the header container (`pointer-events: none`) so it never pushes content;
  - while preparing, it shows the read-only field and a visually hidden status;
  - the save and cancel buttons and the `saveLabel` and `cancelLabel` props are removed.
- Focus: Enter and Escape keep or return focus to the name. Leaving the field, including a save started by blur that later fails, leaves focus where the person put it. A delayed Enter save restores focus only if focus is still in the editor or on `body`.
- `docs/foundations/experience.md`: a single-field rename commits on Enter or blur and reverts on Escape, which is the owner's decision. All other editing keeps explicit save, close and discard controls.
- `Disclosure`:
  - the whole row is the target, outdented with a hover bleed;
  - the chevron is first, rotates with `--motion-fast`, and flips in RTL;
  - content is indented to the label;
  - the height animates through React Aria's `--disclosure-panel-height`, with no transition under reduced motion;
  - the panel bleeds by `--disclosure-inset` so focus rings and pills aren't clipped;
  - `indent={false}` is for content that must line up with rows outside the disclosure (navigation pins).
