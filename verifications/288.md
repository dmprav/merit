# Verification record

## Behavioral decisions

- Text and glyphs sit on the container's content edges, and padded hover or selection backgrounds bleed into the gutter. Buttons with a resting fill or border (primary, secondary) are never outdented. This is documented as "Optical alignment" in `foundations.mdx`.
- Tabs:
  - the list is outdented so the first label's text meets the rail edge, and the selected rule spans the label text;
  - once a strip is scrolled backward-able (`data-scrolled`), its leading gutter is clipped so nothing draws outside the rail (mirrored for RTL);
  - long labels truncate to one line; an internal `AnchoredTooltipBase` shows the full label only when cut off, opening on hover after the shared 350ms delay or immediately on keyboard focus, and Escape dismisses it. The label stays the accessible name.
- Scroll panels have a bleed area (negative margin plus padding), so outdented content never scrolls sideways or clips fills, and they no longer reserve a scrollbar gutter.
- DataTable:
  - text and trailing row-action glyphs sit on the heading's edges;
  - row hover and focus fills bleed into the gutter, but header, row and mobile-card rules stay on the content edges, lining up with tab rails and dividers;
  - messages and the empty state start where cell text starts;
  - a leading quiet control in a cell aligns its text to the cell edge automatically.
- Trailing quiet actions in section headers and panel sections, and Button `alignment="end"`, are shifted with `position: relative`, not a negative margin. Their group keeps its intrinsic size, and labels never wrap.
- Shared variables on `.button`, pointer- and size-aware: `--button-end-outdent` and `--button-icon-inset`. Link gains `alignment="start"`.
- The conflict banner outdents only its first action. Message-box chips start on the input text edge. `JudgmentChoice` outdents itself so its chevron ends on the edge.
- Stylelint rejects negative inline margins in feature styles: `margin-inline*`, `margin-left/right`, shorthand inline positions, and `calc` negatives. Two existing feature outdents keep scoped disables with reasons.
