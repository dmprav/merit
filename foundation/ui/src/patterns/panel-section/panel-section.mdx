# Arrange a side panel's sections

Use `PanelSections` and `PanelSection` for the summaries in a supporting pane, such
as an application's details, staff summary, recommendation, and reviews beside the
material being read. The sections share one rhythm, so a panel assembled from
several features still reads as one list of facts.

<Canvas of={PanelSection.SidePanel} />

- **Space between sections** is equal and owned by `PanelSections`; features do not
  add their own margins, padding, or dividers between sections.
- **The header row** holds the label, an optional count, and the section's quiet
  actions at the end, such as an edit button, an **Add** menu, or a link to the full
  list. The row stays one control tall with or without actions.
- **A supporting line** under the header, such as a status summary of the content,
  goes in `meta` rather than between the header and the content's actions.
- **Content** starts flush with the label. Only a person's row, such as a
  `Judgment`, indents its body under the avatar.

Each section is a region named by its heading. Use `SectionHeader` directly for the
sections of a main page or document, where headings carry more weight.
