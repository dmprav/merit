# Explain a status qualification

`StatusIndicator` is a [toggletip](?path=/docs/components-toggletip--docs) preset for a
status qualification. Place `StatusIndicator` after the value it qualifies, such as review progress or
an earlier recommendation. Keep the status itself visible and give the icon a
specific accessible label. The feature owns the meaning and localized explanation.

<Canvas of={Status.Default} />

The explanation appears once, on hover or keyboard focus, and stays open when
pressed or tapped. Escape and outside press dismiss it. Keep the explanation to one or two sentences;
substantial evidence belongs in the owning workflow.

<Canvas of={Status.Keyboard} />
<Canvas of={Status.Explanation} />

Use warning for a qualification that needs attention and muted for neutral
supporting context. Keep consequences that change an editing decision visible
beside that action instead of hiding them in an indicator.
