# Read and edit a funding recommendation

A manager reading an application needs to know what each person proposed and
which submission they saw. Put the author, funding position, proposed amount,
and any qualification about the basis together. When the application has changed,
show that beside the judgment, where someone scanning the outcome will see it.
**Compare changes** should open the recorded basis. If the
[basis is unknown](?path=/story/features-applications-application-detail--unknown-basis),
say so and leave the comparison unavailable.

## Show what saving this draft would mean

Place the proposed allocation and its effect on the round together. A manager
can adjust the amount and see the remaining budget or overage before saving.
Show when a conditional recommendation counts in full or a deferral reserves
nothing. **Save recommendation** records a proposal. The person can consult the
submission and assessments throughout the edit.
