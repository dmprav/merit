# Record and read a committee decision

A committee decides outside Merit. The manager records what it decided so the
round can account for committed funding, and so the decision stays apart from
the recommendation that proposed it. Show the outcome, the amount, the reason,
the body that decided and when, then who recorded it and when. Those are
different facts: the committee decided, a person recorded.

<Canvas of={Decision.SummaryWithHistory} story={{ inline: false, height: "620px" }} />

## Keep the proposal beside the decision

The first decision starts from the manager's recommendation, and the editor
shows that recommendation for comparison. A decision needs no recommendation and
never changes one. Say so when none exists.

<Canvas of={Decision.RecordFirstDecision} story={{ inline: false, height: "620px" }} />

## Show what saving would commit

Place the budget effect beside the amount. Funding and conditional funding count
in full; a deferral or decline reserves nothing. Show the excess before saving, and
say plainly when the round has no budget, because Merit will not record funding
that the budget cannot hold.

<Canvas of={Decision.OverBudget} story={{ inline: false, height: "620px" }} />

## Ask why a decision changed

Every outcome can change, including a final one, and each change is a new version.
Ask for the explanation once a decision exists and keep it with the version it
explains. If someone else changed the decision first, keep the draft and offer to
reaffirm it against the latest version or discard it.
