# Edit a committee decision

## Show what saving would commit

Place the budget effect beside the amount. Funding and conditional funding count
in full; a deferral or decline reserves nothing. Show the excess before saving, and
say plainly when the round has no budget, because Merit will not record funding
that the budget cannot hold.

<Canvas of={Decision.OverBudget} story={{ inline: false, height: "620px" }} />

## Ask why a decision changed

Every outcome can change, including a final one, and each change is a new version.
Ask for the explanation once a decision exists and keep it with the version it
explains. If someone else changed the decision first, keep the draft and offer to
reaffirm it against the latest version or discard it.
